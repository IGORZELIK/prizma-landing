## One topic. Six formats. Every channel.

## What PRIZMA produces

Publishing regularly is no longer enough. Algorithms weigh consistency, topical relevance and how people behave. PRIZMA builds the formats you need from a single topic, each one tuned to its channel.

### Post

A post with a clear message about your product or offer. Image and text fit each other and the channel.

### Reel

A short video with a clear message. Platforms show reels to people who don't follow you yet, so you reach new people without an ad budget.

### Carousel

Complex ideas, broken down clearly. Carousels earn saves and shares. The kind of content people bookmark and come back to.

### Story

Short and current. Stories keep your channels present between the larger posts and are usually seen first.

### Article

An article for search. PRIZMA analyses real search queries and shapes the text around them.

### Avatar

A face for your brand, with no camera and no shoot. The AI avatar delivers your script on video, regularly and at consistent quality.
